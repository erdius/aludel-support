Aludel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Aludel is a chat app for AI models that you run yourself. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: I don't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What I collect

Nothing. The app does not send your chats, files, settings, server addresses or API keys to me or to any server I operate. I have no way to see them.

Where your information goes

  • Your own server. When you chat, your messages, any images or file excerpts you include, and your chat settings are sent to the AI server whose address you enter. The app asks you to confirm this when you add a server. Aludel does not include or operate any AI service. That server belongs to you or whoever runs it, and its privacy practices apply. If you enter the address of a cloud AI service, that provider receives this data and its own privacy policy applies; I receive none of it.
  • Your search server. If you turn on web search, your search terms go to the SearXNG address you enter. The app may also fetch web pages that the model chooses to read, directly from your device over HTTPS.
  • Tailscale. If you reach your server through Tailscale, your traffic travels over your own Tailscale network. Tailscale's privacy policy applies to that service.
  • iCloud. If iCloud is on, your chats, projects, memories, presets, files and server list sync through your private iCloud database, and your API keys sync through iCloud Keychain. This data is protected by your Apple account and is not accessible to me. You can turn syncing off in the app's iCloud Sync settings.
  • Apple. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; I never receive your payment details.

Files, photos and voice

Documents you add are read on your device, including text recognition for scanned pages, and the search index for them stays on your device. Photos and camera images are used only when you attach them to a message. Microphone and speech recognition are used only when you tap the microphone or turn on hands-free mode, and recognition runs entirely on your device: your voice is not sent to me, to Apple or to your server. Only the text it produces is used, like text you typed.

Memory

The memory feature stores short notes you or the model add, on your device and in your iCloud if sync is on. You can view, edit and delete them in the app. Private chats do not use or save memories.

Your choices

  • Delete any chat, project, file or memory in the app.
  • Turn iCloud sync off in the app, or manage the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data. Data in iCloud remains until you delete it there.

Children

Aludel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
