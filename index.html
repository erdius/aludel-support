Connecting to your server

  • Aludel works with servers that speak the OpenAI-style API, such as llama.cpp, Ollama and LM Studio.
  • Enter the full address with its port, for example http://192.168.1.50:9932. Plain http:// works on your home network; anywhere else use https://.
  • To reach your server away from home with Tailscale, install Tailscale on your computer and your iPhone, then add your computer's Tailscale name (it ends in .ts.net) as the Remote address, for example http://my-mac.tailnet-name.ts.net:9932. Use the name, not the 100.x number.
